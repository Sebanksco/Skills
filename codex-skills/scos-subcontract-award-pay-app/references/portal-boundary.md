# SCOS portal boundary

The portal may accept document uploads and return an editable AI draft. The authenticated SCOS routes remain the only authority for records and previews.

## Allowed without a separate authorization

- Read the active project and available Buyout/commitment choices.
- Upload evidence to the review-only AI endpoint.
- Display comparison results, warnings, blockers, and provenance.
- Edit the unsaved draft.
- Generate a clearly marked draft preview.

## Explicitly prohibited for AI

- Selecting the final bidder.
- Saving or awarding a subcontract.
- Marking a blocker resolved.
- Drafting changed termination language without the base subcontract.
- Signing, binding, issuing, emailing, approving, uploading to SharePoint, writing SQL directly, posting to QBO, or authorizing payment.

Enable the Notice of Award preview only when required documents and decisions pass. Keep agreement preparation disabled until the user confirms bidder selection, approved amount, accepted scope/exclusions, and exact project lineage.
